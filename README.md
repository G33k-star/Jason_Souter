# Jason Souter
Electrical Engineering student interested in embedded systems, robotics, and intelligent sensing. I’m interested in building physical systems that sense their environment, transform measurements into information, process that information in real time, make decisions, and actuate in response. My interests span embedded computing, sensor systems, digital signal processing, electronics, electromagnetics and RF, control systems, and autonomous systems, with an emphasis on understanding the complete physical-world-to-action pipeline. I’m particularly interested in the engineering layers that connect physical phenomena to intelligent behavior: sensors and instrumentation, analog signal acquisition, digital signal processing, embedded computation, decision-making, feedback control, and actuation. I enjoy exploring systems where hardware, firmware, algorithms, and physical behavior have to work together as a single system, including wearable devices, drones, autonomous machines, robotic platforms, and other nontraditional robotic systems.
