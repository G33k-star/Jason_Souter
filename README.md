# Jason Souter
Electrical Engineering student interested in developing intelligent cyber-physical systems that sense physical and multimodal phenomena, process information in real time, make decisions, and respond through control and actuation. My interests span embedded systems and computing, digital signal processing, robotics and autonomous systems, electromagnetic and optical sensing, electronics, FPGA/firmware development, and multiphysics modeling. I’m particularly interested in how sensing hardware, embedded electronics, firmware, signal-processing algorithms, and control systems can be integrated into practical intelligent machines, including UAV/UGV platforms, wearable technologies, and other autonomous, sensor-driven systems. I’m currently building my foundation through coursework, hands-on projects, and independent exploration, with a long-term goal of contributing to the development of intelligent systems.
